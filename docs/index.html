三定律 · The Three Laws

① 有籍

Registry at Birth

出生即登记：身份、创造者、治理归属。

② 有证

Evidence for Every Key Act

关键行为留证据：日志、风险登记、变更记录。

③ 有门禁

Gates Before Every Evolution

触发 → 评估 → 放行 → 复盘，签发权在人。

摘要 · Abstract

EN — The Lifecycle Governance Doctrine (LGD) holds that governance of any autonomous system must be a full-lifecycle chain — birth to retirement — not single-point checks. Three laws: Registry at birth (identity, creator, governance ownership), Evidence for every key act (logs, risk register, change records), Gates before every evolution (trigger → assess → authorize → review; humans hold sign-off). Reference model: medical-device lifecycle regulation, generalized across 12 domains.

中文 — 全程治理论（LGD）主张：凡自治之物——AI、自动驾驶、机器人、低空系统——治理必须是覆盖出生到退役的全生命周期链条，而非单点检查。三律：有籍、有证、有门禁。母版来自医疗器械全生命周期监管，已推广至 12 个域。
